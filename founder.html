Meet our founder

A conviction that businesses grow when their people and systems grow together.

CEO & Founder · People & Organizational Culture Coach

Kazungu Gerald (KG)

Kazungu Gerald founded KG MULTISECTOR LIMITED on a simple conviction: businesses grow when people and systems grow together. He leads the group’s Plumbing Works and Corporate Training divisions and works directly with organizations on leadership, culture and executive management.

His work anchors the group’s promise — Diversified Solutions, Unified Excellence — by keeping one standard of delivery across the company’s areas of focus.

  • People & Organizational Culture Coach
  • Corporate Training & Leadership Development
  • Business Strategy & Executive Advisory
