About KG MULTISECTOR LIMITED

One company. Two specialist divisions.

KG MULTISECTOR LIMITED is a Ugandan company bringing Plumbing Works and Corporate Training together under one commitment to professional service. We work with individuals, businesses and institutions, tailoring each engagement to the need at hand.

From dependable plumbing installations and water systems to leadership development and organisational culture, our teams focus on practical work and lasting progress.

Vision

To become a trusted Ugandan company recognised for excellent plumbing services and impactful corporate training.

Mission

To deliver reliable plumbing solutions and practical learning experiences that help people, businesses and institutions thrive.

Values

Integrity, quality, professionalism, customer focus, innovation, teamwork and continuous growth guide everything we do.
