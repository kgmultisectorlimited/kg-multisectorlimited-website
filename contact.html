Frequently asked questions

Which services does KG MULTISECTOR LIMITED offer?

We focus on Plumbing Works and Corporate Training. Plumbing services include installations, repairs, maintenance and water systems. Training supports leadership, organisational culture, team building and professional development.

Where are you based and where do you work?

Our office is in Kampala, Uganda. Contact us with your location and details of the plumbing work or training you need so we can confirm how we may assist.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the enquiry form above and select Plumbing Works. Describe the work, property or site location, and any relevant requirements. We’ll review the details and discuss the next steps with you.

How quickly will I get a response?

We aim to respond as soon as possible. Include your contact details and enough information about your enquiry to help us direct it to the right team.

Can you help with needs across both divisions?

Yes. Tell us if your enquiry involves both Plumbing Works and Corporate Training, and we’ll help connect you with the relevant teams.

Do you work with small businesses as well as large organisations?

We welcome enquiries from individuals, small businesses, institutions and larger organisations. Let us know what you need so we can understand how best to help.
