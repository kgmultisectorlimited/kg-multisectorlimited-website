01 · PLUMBING WORKS

Plumbing Works Division

Our Plumbing Works Division provides plumbing installations, maintenance, repairs and specialised solutions for residential, commercial, industrial and agricultural environments.

Service areas

  • Residential plumbing and domestic pipework
  • Industrial pipe fitting and water supply
  • Sanitary fittings and connections
  • Drainage and wastewater systems
  • Septic tanks, bio-digesters and soak pits
  • Stormwater and sewer lines
  • Farm water distribution and livestock systems
  • Rainwater harvesting and water storage
  • Water pump installation
  • Boreholes, wells, fountains and springs
  • Repairs, upgrades and maintenance
  • Specialised and customised pipework

02 · CORPORATE TRAINING

Corporate Training Division

Our Corporate Training Division focuses on developing people, strengthening leadership and building productive organisational cultures. Training is for businesses, institutions, organisations and individuals.

Training areas

  • People and organisational culture
  • Culture assessment and development
  • Leadership effectiveness and coaching
  • Management and personal leadership
  • Team building and team cohesion
  • Communication and collaboration
  • Personal and professional development
  • Corporate training and capacity building

Ways we work together

Engagements may include corporate workshops and seminars, leadership programmes, culture assessments, team-building sessions and tailored training. Our approach combines practical learning, interaction, reflection and actionable strategies.
